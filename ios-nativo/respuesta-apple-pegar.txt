2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE

Zentra Movil is the mobile client of Zentra, a business management system
(ERP) used by small and medium retail businesses in Paraguay: grocery stores,
hardware stores, bakeries and similar.

These businesses keep sales, stock and customer accounts on a computer in the
back office, while the owner or clerk is on the shop floor or at a customer's
home. The app gives them the same data on the phone: record a sale in front of
the customer, check real stock before promising a delivery, look up what a
customer owes.

The audience is the business owner and their employees. It is not a consumer
app, and not an internal app for one company: Zentra is sold as a subscription
to any business, and anyone can sign up from the app itself.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES

- Supabase, self-hosted at api.neura.com.py: authentication and database (our
  own server, not supabase.com).
- Zentra ERP API at sistemas.neura.com.py: our own backend; business data and
  the WhatsApp relay below.
- Zentra billing server at cobros.neura.com.py: our own server; verifies App
  Store receipts and activates the subscription.
- Apple StoreKit 2: all in-app purchases. No other payment channel on iOS.
- Firebase Cloud Messaging: push notifications only.
- WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta): each business connects its OWN WhatsApp
  Business account; we do not provide it.

No AI services, no ad networks, no analytics SDKs, no data providers.

User-generated content: the messaging screen is not a social feature. It shows
the WhatsApp conversations between a business and its own customers, through
that business's own WhatsApp Business account. There is no feed, no public
profiles, no discovery, and no way for one app user to contact another. A
clerk only sees conversations of the shop they work for. There is therefore no
community to moderate and no in-app reporting or blocking: blocking a number
is done in WhatsApp by the business that owns it. If App Review still requires
a reporting mechanism here, we will add it - please let us know.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES

None. Same features and content in every region, Spanish only. Prices are set
per region by App Store Connect; everything else is identical.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
